Sales and Inventory Analysis – Fruit Dataset (Power BI Dashboard)

Live Dashboard:

Project Overview

This project is a Power BI dashboard created to analyze sales and inventory data for a fruit and vegetable dataset.
The dashboard focuses on sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product pricing, discounts, and inventory insights.

The goal of this project is to help understand which products sell the most and least, customer spending patterns, and pricing differences across product categories.

What This Dashboard Shows

Total sales amount

Total quantity sold

Total discount given

Most purchased quantity by customer

Sales amount by gender and birth year

Top 3 most sold products

Top 3 least sold products

Top 3 most expensive products

Unit price comparison by product and category

Relationship between quantity, total amount, and unit price

Key Insights

A small group of customers contributes a higher purchase quantity

Sales amounts vary across gender and different birth years

Asparagus and Rhubarb appear among the top-selling and highest-priced products

Some products have high prices but lower sales volume

Fruits and vegetables show clear differences in pricing and sales patterns

Discounts play a role in total sales value

Visualizations Used

KPI cards for total sales, quantity sold, and discount given

Bar charts for customer-wise purchase quantity

Area and line charts for sales by gender and birth year

Donut charts for top and least sold products

Scatter plot for quantity vs total amount and unit price

Column charts for unit price by product and category
